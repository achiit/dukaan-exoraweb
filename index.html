About

Built by someone who lives here.

I started nexora.web in 2025 because too many local shops and service people in Guwahati were stuck with a Facebook page and a number written on a wall. I wanted to build sites that a neighbour could find on Google and trust.

I keep things simple. You tell me what you do, I ask a few questions over WhatsApp, and you get a site that loads fast on a normal 4G connection. No jargon, no surprise bills.

I still remember the first client who called me back to say a customer had found them on Google and walked in the same day. That is the result I care about.
